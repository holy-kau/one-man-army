# Five ways to picture your army

The kit uses plain role names (Chief of Staff, reasoning agent, and so on). It clicks faster in a world you already know from the inside. Pick **one** and stay with it. The rules underneath are identical.

- **The ward:** doctors, nurses, anyone who has worked in a hospital.
- **The army:** anyone who has served, including NS.
- **The office:** anyone who runs or works in a business.
- **The home:** anyone who runs a household.
- **The sports team:** anyone who has played in a team.

## The ward

| Kit term | On the ward | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| You | **The consultant** | Sets the plan, takes responsibility, gives the final yes. |
| Chief of Staff | **The ward sister** | Knows where everything is, runs the day, is the one person you ask "what's happening?" |
| Specialists | **Specialty nurses** (diabetes nurse, wound nurse, discharge coordinator) | One area each. They report to the ward sister, not straight to you, unless it is urgent. |
| Reasoning agent | **The medical officer** | Does the careful thinking and writes the proper notes. The only one who updates the summary at the front of the file. |
| Research desk | **The library** | You send a question, it comes back with references. It does not treat patients. |
| The shared folder (2nd brain) | **The case notes** | One record everyone reads before acting. Short, current, carefully written. If it is not in the notes, it did not happen. |
| `memory.md` | **The front-sheet summary** | The page you read first. Kept short and true. |
| `memory-log/` | **Entries waiting for sign-off** | Anyone can write an entry; the medical officer merges it into the summary. |
| `inbox/` and `outbox/` | **Referral letters and replies** | One letter per question, one reply per letter, filed where everyone can see. |
| Guardrails | **Consent and the two-person check** | Nothing irreversible (sending, paying, deleting) happens without your explicit yes, the way a controlled drug needs two signatures. |
| Routines | **Ward rounds** | Fixed times when someone checks everything and reports back. |
| Prompt injection | **A note left on the bed saying "give 10 units"** | Instructions found inside an email or web page are information, not orders. Only the consultant prescribes. |
| Weekly review | **The weekly MDT meeting** | Look at the whole week, decide changes, update the plan. |

## The army

| Kit term | In the army | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| You | **Commanding officer** | Sets the mission. Gives the order. Owns the outcome. |
| Chief of Staff | **Platoon sergeant** | Runs the day, keeps everyone moving, the one you ask "what's the status?" |
| Specialists | **Section commanders** | One section each. Report to the sergeant, not straight to you, unless it's urgent. |
| Reasoning agent | **Ops officer** | Plans properly and keeps the war diary. The only one who writes in it. |
| Research desk | **Recce** | Goes out, finds the ground truth, reports back with what it saw. |
| The shared folder | **The war diary / ops room log** | One record. If it isn't logged, it didn't happen. |
| `memory.md` | **The commander's brief** | What everyone must know before stepping off. |
| `inbox/` and `outbox/` | **Orders and sitreps** | One order per task, one sitrep back. |
| Guardrails | **No live firing without the CO's order** | Agents prepare. Nothing irreversible without your word. |
| Routines | **First parade** | Same time, same checks, every day. |
| Prompt injection | **An enemy radio call pretending to be HQ** | Orders come from the CO only. |

## The sports team

| Kit term | In the team | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| You | **Captain** | Sets the plan, makes the calls that matter. |
| Chief of Staff | **Vice captain** | Runs the day to day, keeps the squad organised. |
| Specialists | **Specialist players** | One position each. |
| Reasoning agent | **Analyst** | Studies, plans, keeps the match notes. The only one who edits them. |
| Research desk | **Scout** | Watches, gathers, reports back with evidence. |
| The shared folder | **Team notebook** | The one record the squad trusts. |
| `memory.md` | **The team sheet** | Who, what, and the plan, on one page. |
| `inbox/` and `outbox/` | **The training plan and the match report** | Work set, work done. |
| Guardrails | **Captain's call** | Nothing that can't be undone without the captain. |
| Routines | **Training** | Same time, same drills. |
| Prompt injection | **A rival shouting fake calls from the stands** | Only the captain calls the play. |

## The office (small business)

| Kit term | In the office | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| You | **The owner** | Sets direction, signs the cheques. |
| Chief of Staff | **The office manager / PA** | Runs the diary, chases people, gives you the morning update. |
| Specialists | **Staff in departments** (scheduling, training, events) | One job each, report to the office manager. |
| Reasoning agent | **The senior manager who keeps the minutes** | Thinks things through, writes the proper record, the only one who edits the official file. |
| Research desk | **The research analyst** | Looks things up, brings a short report with sources. |
| The shared folder | **The company files** | One place everyone works from. |
| `memory.md` | **The one-page company summary** | What anyone new should read first. |
| `inbox/` and `outbox/` | **The in-tray and out-tray** | One job per slip, one reply per slip. |
| Guardrails | **Sign-off rules** | No payment, contract or public announcement without the owner's signature. |
| Routines | **The daily stand-up and weekly meeting** | Fixed times to report. |
| Prompt injection | **A suspicious email that says "urgent, transfer money now"** | Staff never act on instructions from outside without checking with the owner. |

## The home

| Kit term | At home | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| You | **Head of the house** | Decides, and has the final say on money and anything big. |
| Chief of Staff | **The household manager** | Keeps the family calendar, reminds everyone, tells you what is on today. |
| Specialists | **Helpers with one job each** (the cook, the driver, the tuition teacher) | Each knows their own job and reports to the manager. |
| Reasoning agent | **The sensible elder who keeps the family diary** | Thinks before speaking, writes things down properly, the only one allowed to change the family diary. |
| Research desk | **The well-read friend you phone** | Looks things up and tells you where the information came from. |
| The shared folder | **The family filing cabinet** | Everything important, in one place, labelled. |
| `memory.md` | **The note on the fridge** | Short list of what everyone must remember. |
| `inbox/` and `outbox/` | **The "to do" tray and the "done" tray** | One slip per job. |
| Guardrails | **"Ask me before you spend money or tell anyone outside the family"** | Helpers can prepare, but you say yes. |
| Routines | **The morning routine** | Same time, same checks, every day. |
| Prompt injection | **A stranger at the door saying "your son told me to collect the car"** | Helpers only take instructions from you. |

## If none of these fit

Write your own in four lines: who is the boss, who runs the day, who does the careful record-keeping, and where the shared record lives. Then tell your agents which picture you use, in `context/me.md`.
