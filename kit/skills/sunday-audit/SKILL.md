# Sunday audit

## Steps
1. **Score the week** against the aims in `memory.md`: what moved, what didn't.
2. **One agent, one job:** flag any agent that overlapped another or drifted from its card. Suggest a merge or a cut.
3. **Sweep the files:** delete stale lines (past dates, finished items); merge duplicates; turn real contradictions into questions in `memory-log/QUESTIONS-YYYYMMDD.md`; keep `memory.md` short.
4. **Merge** the week's `memory-log/` entries and move them to `memory-log/done/`.
5. **Collect** the "Lessons and spec updates" bullets from the week's RESULT files into one `PROPOSAL-YYYYMMDD-spec-updates.md`.
6. **List** finished inbox and outbox files to move to `done/`. Move nothing out of the folder without a yes.
7. **File** `outbox/RESULT-sunday-audit-YYYYMMDD.md`: top 3 for next week, questions for [YOUR_NAME].

## Off limits
- Never delete. Replace a file by renaming the old one `-retired-YYYYMMDD` and moving it to `_archive/`.
- Never settle a contradiction by guessing.

## Done looks like
- [ ] No open contradictions, or each is a written question.
- [ ] Next week's top 3 set.
- [ ] RESULT filed.
