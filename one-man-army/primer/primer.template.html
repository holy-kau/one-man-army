    Safety comes first. Always.

    Speed is never worth a mistake you can't undo. Every agent in this kit follows five rules before anything else:

    1. You hold the pen. Agents draft. Nothing is sent, posted, paid, booked or deleted without your yes.
    2. Sensitive stays offline. Money, IDs, passwords and anyone's medical details never go into an AI tool or a synced folder.
    3. Links are suspect. Agents never click links or open attachments in email, even from people you trust.
    4. When in doubt, stop. An agent that isn't sure asks you. It never guesses.
    5. Least access. Each agent sees only what its one job needs, and you can switch any of it off in a minute.

    Modelled on the published privacy and trustworthy AI principles of the biggest names in tech: collect less, keep sensitive work on your own device, show you everything, lock it all down. Full rules in guardrails.md.

    Thirty minutes. Then it works for you.

      The 10 minute habit

      1. Morning, 3 min. Read the brief.
      2. Morning, 2 min. Answer the one decision it asks.
      3. Midday, 3 min. File one task with a three line brief.
      4. Evening, 2 min. “Check the outbox.” Yes, no, or redo.

      The 4 week ramp

      1. Week 1. Chief of Staff, reasoning agent, morning brief. Nothing else.
      2. Week 2. Add one specialist. Write every task as a brief.
      3. Week 3. Add the outbox checks and the Saturday digest.
      4. Week 4. First weekly review. Cut what you don't use.

      Rollback and approval

      Agents draft; you press send. Anything that can't be undone (send, pay, book, delete, post) waits for your yes. Nothing is deleted: finished work moves to a done/ folder, and your drive keeps version history, so any change can be rolled back. Patient and client data never goes into cloud AI. A local redaction add-on is planned, not built.

      What you would share, and what you never share

      Collected only if you tick the box and press send:

      • Your answers from step 1, as fixed choices (for example "healthcare", "phone and laptop", "Claude"), not your name. Age, sex and location are never sent.
      • Which tools you picked for which roles, and which picture you chose.
      • Each week, if you choose: counts of routines and tasks your agents ran, and a short check-in.
      • A random tester code (yours: ) so weeks can be linked without your name.

      Never collected:

      • Your name, email, phone, timezone or location.
      • Any patient, client or other person's details.
      • What your tasks were about, or any message or file content.
      • Health details, money figures, passwords or account numbers.

      Where it goes: a form in the kit author's Jotform account, which only he can open. It is used only to improve the kit, never sold, and deleted on request. Jotform also has its own privacy terms. Be aware: with only a handful of testers, he may be able to guess who sent what, which is why nothing you would mind him seeing is ever asked.

      How to stop: simply don't send. To remove what you sent, tell him your tester code and it will be deleted. Usage logging in your kit is switched off by one line in AGENTS.md.
