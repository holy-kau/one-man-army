# One Man Army kit v1: scope

What this version is, what it is not, and what might come next.

## In v1

- A clean, installable starter: rulebook, guardrails, routines, memory and context templates, and the inbox / outbox / memory-log / roadmap folders.
- An interactive primer (a single web page) that asks about current AI use, teaches the basics, recommends tools, and builds a personalised copy of the kit in the browser.
- Works with any of Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok Bot or Muse, alone or together (`tools.md`).
- Three pictures of the team: ward, office, household (`analogies.md`).
- A practice task and a practice result, so you can test the loop on day one.
- Seven role cards: Chief of Staff, reasoning agent, Research desk, Scheduler, Learning, Health, Lifestyle.
- The approval rules: draft by default; irreversible actions need an explicit yes; one decision at a time; no secrets or patient data in the shared folder; money detail stays offline.
- An **opt-in** usage log (counts only) and a weekly check-in, both off by default.

## Deliberately out of v1

- Anyone's live memory, calendar, contacts, or private working files.
- A ready-made shared drive to copy. You put the kit in a folder you own.
- A trading or portfolio agent, a marketing or brand agent, a stylist, or social-media scouts.
- A large skills library, install scripts, or one-click connectors. v1 ships three starter skills only: interview me, the Sunday audit, and make a skill.
- Finance trackers, payroll, invoicing, or any money figures.
- Clinical workflows, clinic back-office agents, or anything that touches patient records.
- Employer-specific or regulator-specific filing, other than the general reminder in `guardrails.md`.

These were left out on purpose so a friend can install the kit without inheriting someone else's life, and without being handed something that looks more finished than it is.

## Ideas for v2 (to be shaped by tester feedback)

1. **A copy-ready folder and a guided interview.** One "make a copy" starting point, plus a 15-minute Chief of Staff interview that writes the first `memory.md` and `context/me.md`.
2. **An inbox processor skill.** Turn a messy chat, a voice note or a pile of open loops into well-formed `TASK-*.md` files, and close the loop back into memory on Sunday.
3. **Local redaction (planned, not built).** A step that runs on your own computer and strips names, ID numbers and other identifiers before text reaches any cloud AI tool.
4. **Optional add-on packs,** kept separate from the core: a connector pack (calendar, email drafts) switched on only once the core loop is stable, and role packs for specific professions.

## Sharing and selling

A free share of this template with friends is fine.

If this kit is ever sold, the owner's employer declaration comes first. Do not treat v1 as a product launch. It is a prototype for review and a starter for people who already trust the author.
