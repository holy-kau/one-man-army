# Starter roadmap: the 4 week ramp

Ten minutes a day beats a weekend of setup. Adapt or ignore it.

## Week 1: the core

- Fill the placeholders in `AGENTS.md`, `memory.md` and `context/me.md`.
- Stand up Chief of Staff and the reasoning agent.
- Turn on the 07:00 morning brief.
- Run the practice task in `inbox/`. Confirm a result lands in `outbox/`.
- Start the 10 minute habit (`playbook.md`).

## Week 2: one specialist

- Add one specialist you'll actually use (often Learning or Scheduler). One line job, short no go list.
- Write every task as a three line brief: goal, done looks like, don't touch.
- Before handing over a workflow, fix the broken step first.

## Week 3: the rhythm

- Turn on the 13:15 and 19:15 outbox checks.
- Turn on the Saturday digest and the Sunday calendar sync.

## Week 4: review and cut

- Let the reasoning agent do one memory merge and the first weekly review.
- Cut any agent, routine or file you didn't use.
- Only then consider a second specialist, or the Research desk.

Done when the morning brief is useful on a weekday, and at least one real task has gone inbox → outbox → one line report.
