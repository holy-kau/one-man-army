# The playbook

Seven rules that make the kit work. Read once. Come back when something feels off.

## 1. Start with what you care about, not the tools

Pick the things that drain you and the things you want more time for. Build agents for those. The tool is the least important decision you'll make.

Doctors: you're the consultant, your agents are the ward team, and a chief of staff runs them. Everyone else: see `analogies.md` for your world.

## 2. One agent, one job

Each agent gets one line of job and a short no go list. Overlap is where mistakes hide.

| Agent | One line job | No go |
|---|---|---|
| Chief of Staff | Runs the day. Every morning asks: "Does [YOUR_NAME] need to know anything?" | Never sends, pays, books or deletes. |
| Reasoning agent | Thinks it through and keeps the record. The only one who edits memory. | Never acts outside the folder. |
| Learning (study) | Turns study into small, planned blocks. | Never invents a source. |
| Scheduler (admin) | Guards your time and drafts the admin. | Never accepts work or sends a message for you. |
| Health | Coaches sleep, training and habits. | Never diagnoses, prescribes or books. |

Start with Chief of Staff and the reasoning agent. Add one specialist at a time.

## 3. Write the brief, then let it run

Every task gets three lines:

- **Goal:** what should be true when it's done.
- **Done looks like:** the thing you'll be able to see (a table, a draft, a list).
- **Don't touch:** what is off limits (your calendar, a person, your weekends).

Then step back. A good brief is like a good referral letter or handover: clear enough that nobody has to call you back. `inbox/_TEMPLATE.md` already has these three lines.

## 4. Safe by default: rollback and approval

- **Agents draft. You press send.** Email, messages and posts are drafts until you say yes.
- **Anything irreversible waits for your yes:** send, pay, book, delete, post, accept.
- **Nothing gets deleted.** Finished tasks move to `done/` folders. Your drive keeps version history, so any change can be rolled back.
- **Patient and client data never goes into cloud AI.** Not even "just this once".
- **Future add-on (not built):** a local redaction step that strips identifiers on your own computer before anything reaches an AI tool.

## 5. Ride it every day

**The 10 minute habit**

1. Morning, 3 min: read the brief.
2. Morning, 2 min: answer the one decision it asks.
3. Midday, 3 min: file one task with a three line brief.
4. Evening, 2 min: "Check the outbox." Yes, no, or redo.

**The 4 week ramp** (detail in `roadmap/starter.md`)

- Week 1: Chief of Staff, reasoning agent, morning brief. Nothing else.
- Week 2: add one specialist. Write every task as a brief.
- Week 3: add the outbox checks and the Saturday digest.
- Week 4: first weekly review. Cut what you don't use.

## 6. Redesign, then automate

Fix a broken workflow before you hand it to an agent. An agent running a bad process just fails faster.

## 7. Stay human

Agents draft. You decide. You speak in your own voice. The relationships, the judgement and the signature stay yours.
